# About Meridian Homes

Made-up test agency for this build. All listings, people and payments are test data.

## The agency

- Lagos property agency with two branches: **Lekki Phase 1** (Admiralty Way) and **Yaba** (Herbert Macaulay Way).
- 40 homes, a mix of rentals (priced per year, as is normal in Lagos) and sales.
- Lekki covers Lekki Phase 1, Ikate, Chevron, Osapa London, Agungi and Ologolo. Yaba covers Alagomeji, Sabo, Akoka, Onike, Jibowu, Iwaya and Adekunle.
- Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 17:00 WAT. Closed Sunday.

## Viewings

- Monday to Friday: 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00 WAT. Saturday: 10:00 to 14:00. No Sunday viewings.
- Book at least 1 hour ahead and up to 30 days ahead.
- An agent meets the buyer at the property. Bring a valid ID (NIN slip, driver's licence, international passport or voter's card).
- Viewings last about 30 minutes.

## The ₦5,000 visit fee

- Why it exists: too many viewings were no-shows, and agents' time was wasted. The fee secures the slot and pays for the agent's trip.
- Paid by card on a Paystack checkout page. The link comes from the assistant. It is valid while the slot is held (15 minutes).
- Refund policy:
  - Buyer cancels 24 or more hours before the viewing: full refund.
  - Buyer cancels less than 24 hours before: fee kept.
  - Buyer doesn't turn up: fee kept.
  - The agency cancels, or the home is let or sold before the viewing: full refund.
  - Payment arrives after the hold lapsed and someone else has taken the slot: full refund, automatically.
- It is not a deposit, it doesn't reduce the rent or price, and it isn't an agency or legal fee.

## Words buyers use

- "NEPA", "light", "power": grid electricity. Our data records the grid **power band** (Band A = 20+ hours a day, as reported to the agency), plus inverter, solar and generator where recorded.
- "BQ": boys' quarters, a separate staff room or flat on the compound.
- "Service charge": yearly estate charge for security, waste, shared power and so on.
- "C of O", "Governor's Consent", "Deed of Assignment": title documents. You may state which document is recorded. Whether a title is genuine or safe is a legal question for the coordinator.
- "Agreement and commission", "legal fee", "caution fee": rent-related fees. Out of scope; the coordinator handles them.

## The coordinator

- A member of staff who reviews handed-over chats, answers logged questions and manages visits.
- Hand over in this chat. Never give a personal phone number or promise a response time.
